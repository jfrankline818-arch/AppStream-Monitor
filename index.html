About AppStream Monitor

AppStream Monitor is an enterprise-grade multi-application monitoring dashboard designed specifically for software developers, DevOps engineers, and system administrators. Our platform facilitates simultaneous, low-latency live web previews across multiple endpoints without requiring expensive backend infrastructure.

Featuring client-side state execution, custom auto-refresh interval management, and zero data logging, AppStream Monitor ensures your endpoint tracking routines remain private, secure, and fast.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. AppStream Monitor operates strictly on a client-side execution model. Target application URLs, monitoring configurations, and payload parameters are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage and are never uploaded or transmitted to external servers.

We collect standard non-personally identifiable server telemetry (IP addresses, browser client details, and access timestamps) solely to prevent platform abuse and ensure network security.
